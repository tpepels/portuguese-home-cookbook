## Visual direction

All photographs should match the existing set:

- photorealistic food photography
- Portuguese home kitchen, not restaurant plating
- warm natural window light
- rustic wood, linen, blue-and-white ceramics or subtle azulejo accents
- portrait orientation, ideally 4:5
- food fills most of the frame
- no people
- no typography or text baked into the image
- consistent domestic, slightly imperfect presentation
